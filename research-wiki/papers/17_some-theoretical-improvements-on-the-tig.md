# Some theoretical improvements on the tightness of PAC-Bayes risk certificates for neural networks

- **Authors:** Diego García-Pérez, Emilio Parrado-Hernández, John Shawe-Taylor
- **Venue/Year:** arXiv preprint (arXiv:2510.07935) (2025)
- **ID:** arXiv:2510.07935 — ✓ verified
- **Contribution:** Proposes four theoretical refinements that improve the tightness and usability of PAC-Bayes risk certificates for neural networks built on the PBB framework of Pérez-Ortiz et al. (2021); two of the contributions explicitly improve bound tightness, directly extending the fclassic/flambda/fquad objective family we reproduce.
- **Relevance to our RQ:** Primary 'stretch/SOTA' target for our project: the most direct recent theoretical successor to our core baseline. Sets a state-of-the-art bar for certificate tightness that our extensions to Fashion-MNIST/CIFAR-10 should compare against, and informs H1 (whether tighter objective variants beat fclassic at low empirical risk). Same author lineage (Parrado-Hernández, Shawe-Taylor) as the PBB line. ID verified via arXiv abstract page.
