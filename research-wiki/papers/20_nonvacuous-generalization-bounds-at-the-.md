# Non-Vacuous Generalization Bounds at the ImageNet Scale: A PAC-Bayesian Compression Approach

- **Authors:** Wenda Zhou, Victor Veitch, Morgane Austern, Ryan P. Adams, Peter Orbanz
- **Venue/Year:** ICLR 2019; arXiv:1804.05862 (2019)
- **ID:** arXiv:1804.05862 — ✓ verified
- **Contribution:** First non-vacuous generalization guarantees for realistic architectures at ImageNet scale (and CIFAR-10), combining an original PAC-Bayes bound with off-the-shelf network compression; established that compression-based PAC-Bayes could scale to large datasets.
- **Relevance to our RQ:** Foundational SOTA predecessor to Lotfi et al. (2022) and the standard prior baseline for non-vacuous bounds on CIFAR-10/ImageNet. Its bound magnitudes on CIFAR-10 anchor H3 and contextualize how PBB weight-distribution certificates compare to the compression route. ID verified via arXiv abstract page.
