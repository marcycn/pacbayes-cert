# Certified Adversarial Robustness via Randomized Smoothing

- **Authors:** Jeremy M. Cohen, Elan Rosenfeld, J. Zico Kolter
- **Venue/Year:** ICML 2019 (PMLR vol. 97, pp. 1310-1320; arXiv:1902.02918) (2019)
- **ID:** arXiv:1902.02918 — ✓ verified
- **Contribution:** Turns any classifier robust to Gaussian noise into one with a provable L2 certified radius via Neyman-Pearson-based analysis of the noise-averaged prediction; gives a distribution-free certificate over input perturbations (not over the population risk).
- **Relevance to our RQ:** Positioning: a competing 'deterministic certified' method. It certifies local adversarial robustness, NOT population classification risk like PAC-Bayes -- clarifies our distinct guarantee (risk on unseen data) for reviewers asking 'certified vs certified'. Useful contrast for scope framing.
