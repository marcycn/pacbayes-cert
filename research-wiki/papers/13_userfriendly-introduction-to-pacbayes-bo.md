# User-friendly Introduction to PAC-Bayes Bounds

- **Authors:** Pierre Alquier
- **Venue/Year:** arXiv:2110.11216 (Foundations and Trends in Machine Learning, Now Publishers, 2024) (2021)
- **ID:** arXiv:2110.11216 — ✓ verified
- **Contribution:** Modern survey/primer unifying the McAllester, Seeger, Maurer, and Catoni bound families under one change-of-measure framework, giving the explicit closed forms (the sqrt-form 'classic' bound, the Catoni lambda-bound, the kl/Seeger bound) side by side with matching constants. Includes a simplified treatment of Catoni localisation. ID verified via arXiv abstract page; published 2024 in FnT-ML.
- **Relevance to our RQ:** The single best reference for writing the exact bound FORM for fclassic vs flambda vs fquad in our method section. Lets us explain to reviewers why flambda (Catoni lambda) and fquad (quadratic/log-rate) are expected tighter than fclassic, supporting H1, without re-deriving each theorem from the 2007 monograph.
