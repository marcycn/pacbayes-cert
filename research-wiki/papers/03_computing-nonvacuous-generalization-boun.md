# Computing Nonvacuous Generalization Bounds for Deep (Stochastic) Neural Networks with Many More Parameters than Training Data

- **Authors:** Gintare Karolina Dziugaite, Daniel M. Roy
- **Venue/Year:** UAI 2017 (Proceedings of the 33rd Conference on Uncertainty in Artificial Intelligence), paper 173 (arXiv:1703.11008) (2017)
- **ID:** arXiv:1703.11008 — ✓ verified
- **Contribution:** First demonstration that PAC-Bayes bounds can be made numerically NON-VACUOUS for deep (stochastic) neural network classifiers with millions of parameters, reviving the Langford & Caruana (2001) approach. Method: run SGD to find a good weight region, then optimize the variance of an isotropic Gaussian perturbation over the weights (a data-free prior centered at the SGD solution) to minimize a PAC-Bayes (Catoni) generalization bound. On MNIST achieves non-vacuous but loose bounds (best ~0.21 bound at ~88% accuracy, or ~0.65 bound at ~94% accuracy per the Pérez-Ortiz Table 2 reproduction). Official code: github.com/gkdziugaite/pacbayes-opt.
- **Relevance to our RQ:** The immediate precursor whose numbers PBB beats by ~an order of magnitude. Directly the baseline in Pérez-Ortiz 2021 Table 2 (same MNIST FCN 784x600x600x10): PBB fquad reaches cert .0279 vs D&R2017 best .2100. Establishes the two-stage (optimize-perturbation) paradigm that PBB replaces with end-to-end bound minimization. Our data-free-isotropic prior condition is exactly this baseline. Underpins H1/H4 as the 'previous literature' tightened-bound target, and motivates the need for data-dependent priors. Foundational item explicitly named in the project charter.
