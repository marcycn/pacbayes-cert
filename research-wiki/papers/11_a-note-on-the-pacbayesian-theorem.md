# A Note on the PAC-Bayesian Theorem

- **Authors:** Andreas Maurer
- **Venue/Year:** arXiv preprint cs.LG (cs/0411099) (2004)
- **ID:** arXiv:cs/0411099 — ✓ verified
- **Contribution:** Gives a cleaner, slightly strengthened statement of the PAC-Bayes theorem with a self-contained proof via change-of-measure and exponential-moment inequalities for [0,1]-valued iid averages. Provides the precise constants and the standard 'KL + log(2sqrt(n)/delta)' denominator that later papers quote verbatim; source of the tight PAC-Bayes-kl inequality. ID verified via arXiv abstract page.
- **Relevance to our RQ:** The reference most modern PAC-Bayes papers (including the PBB line) cite for the exact constants in fclassic and the PAC-Bayes-kl inversion used to compute risk certificates. Useful when writing the precise bound form for each objective and needing a citable source for the ln(2sqrt(n)/delta) term.
