# Tighter Risk Certificates for Neural Networks

- **Authors:** María Pérez-Ortiz, Omar Rivasplata, John Shawe-Taylor, Csaba Szepesvári
- **Venue/Year:** Journal of Machine Learning Research (JMLR), Vol. 22, Article 227, pp. 1-40 (arXiv:2007.12911) (2021)
- **ID:** arXiv:2007.12911 — ✓ verified
- **Contribution:** The PRIMARY paper we reproduce/extend. Introduces PAC-Bayes-with-Backprop (PBB): train a weight DISTRIBUTION by directly minimizing a PAC-Bayes bound as the loss. Studies three objectives -- fclassic (classic McAllester/Pinsker relaxation of PAC-Bayes-kl), flambda (Thiemann et al. 2017), and fquad (PAC-Bayes-quadratic relaxation) -- plus fbbb (Blundell 2015) and ferm (ERM baseline). Computes risk certificates by inverting the PAC-Bayes-kl bound using part of the training data (n0 of n). Defines 'self-certified learning'. Official code: github.com/mperezortiz/PBB. EXACT MNIST Table 1 targets: FCN+Rand.Init fquad cert .3155/stch .0268, flambda .3275/.0211, fclassic .3304/.0407, fbbb .5516/.0088; FCN+Learnt fquad cert .0279/.0084, flambda .0354/.0082, fclassic .0284/.0101, fbbb .0968/.0063; CNN+Learnt fquad .0155/.0045. ferm FCN .0152, CNN .0092 test error. Table 2 head-to-head: PBB fquad cert .0279 vs D&R2018 SGLD .2100. CIFAR-10 Table 5: 9-layer fquad(70%) cert .2377, 13-layer fquad(70%) .1758, 15-layer flambda(70%) .2121.
