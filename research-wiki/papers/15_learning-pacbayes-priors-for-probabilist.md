# Learning PAC-Bayes Priors for Probabilistic Neural Networks

- **Authors:** María Pérez-Ortiz, Omar Rivasplata, Benjamin Guedj, Matthew Gleeson, Jingyu Zhang, John Shawe-Taylor, Miroslaw Bober, Josef Kittler
- **Venue/Year:** arXiv preprint (also HAL-Inria hal-03351794); AAAI 2022 (2021)
- **ID:** arXiv:2109.10304 — ✓ verified
- **Contribution:** Empirically investigates the role of the prior across 6 datasets, learning data-dependent PAC-Bayes priors on subsets of data with different strategies/amounts. Dissects data into a prior-building subset and a posterior-training subset (the 'transfer subset' recipe, including prior-validation on a small slice); shows the optimal prior-data fraction is dataset-dependent and that reserving a small slice for prior validation helps. Reports e.g. ~2.2% risk certificate vs ~2.1% stochastic test error on MNIST. Compares under- vs over-parameterised models and several prior-training objectives/regularisation strategies.
- **Relevance to our RQ:** STRONG NOVELTY THREAT to H4: this already shows data-dependent/learnt/subset priors beat data-free priors and tightens certificates, including the transfer/reduced-data angle -- H4 is largely pre-empted. Primary source for H4 and for the bound/Dprior/Dtrain data-dissection protocol the project must implement. Residual novelty: our framing ties the prior effect explicitly to the objective (fquad/flambda) AND predictor-rule across a clean 3-dataset ladder, which this preprint does not isolate. Must cite as the primary prior-effect baseline and NOT claim H4 as a first finding.
