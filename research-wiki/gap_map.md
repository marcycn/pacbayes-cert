## Sharpened RQ
Across MNIST, Fashion-MNIST, and CIFAR-10, is the GAP between a PBB network's empirical (training-set) risk and its PAC-Bayes risk certificate — the "certificate slack" — governed more by dataset difficulty (label noise / class overlap) than by the choice of bound objective or prior? Concretely: does the relative ordering of {fclassic, flambda, fquad} x {isotropic, data-dependent, transfer/learned} priors by certificate slack stay constant across all three datasets, or does difficulty *flip the winning configuration* (e.g., does a data-dependent prior stop helping once CIFAR-10's higher irreducible error dominates the KL term)? This is the one question the JMLR 2021 paper cannot answer (it had no FMNIST and did not frame slack as the response variable), it is falsifiable (a constant ordering kills the "difficulty flips the winner" sub-claim; a flipped ordering kills the "objective/prior ordering is stable" sub-claim), and a 2-week MNIST+FMNIST pilot on 1 GPU produces a go/no-go signal before committing to CIFAR-10.

## Recommended angle
"Reframe from 'we extend PBB to Fashion-MNIST and study the tradeoff' (reproduction + new dataset, sub-CCF-B) to: 'How valid is your risk certificate for the predictor you actually deploy? A cross-dataset study of certificate validity for posterior-mean predictors in PAC-Bayes-with-Backprop.' Concretely: (1) reproduce the JMLR 2021 Table 1/2/5 numbers as the honest baseline (Task #9) and explicitly label predictor-rule/objective/prior comparisons as reproduction; (2) ADD Fashion-MNIST as the missing middle rung to turn the MNIST->CIFAR-10 degradation into a 3-point MNIST->Fashion-MNIST->CIFAR-10 ladder with a fitted tradeoff curve (Task #10); (3) make the NOVEL core a 'deployed-cert gap' analysis: show that the PBB Gibbs-risk certificate can be loose or even misleading for the deterministic posterior-mean predictor most users deploy, derive/adapt a variance-aware majority-vote certificate (Lacasse 2006 family) for the fquad/flambda objectives, and measure this gap and its dataset-ordering across the full ladder (Task #11 expanded). This gives one clean, previously-unframed question (is the certificate valid for the deployed predictor?), one genuine empirical gap (Fashion-MNIST), and a measurement + small-theoretical-delta contribution rather than a method invention -- which is the right altitude for a CCF-B venue and cannot be dismissed as 'reproduce + new dataset'."
