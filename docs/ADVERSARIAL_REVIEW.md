## The pivot (the only path to CCF-B)

Reframe from *"extend PBB to Fashion-MNIST and study the tradeoff"* (reproduction + new dataset, sub-CCF-B) to:

> **"How valid is your risk certificate for the predictor you actually deploy? A cross-dataset study of certificate validity for posterior-mean predictors in PAC-Bayes-with-Backprop."**

Three load-bearing moves:
1. **Deployed-predictor certificate validity (THE novelty).** The PBB certificate bounds the *Gibbs/stochastic* risk; the JMLR paper compares predictors on *test error only* and explicitly says "we plan to explore this in future work." No PBB-lineage paper gives a valid, tight certificate for the **deterministic posterior-mean / majority-vote predictor that is actually deployed**. Define a **"deployed-cert gap"** (certificate − true deployed-predictor risk) and measure it across the dataset ladder. Minimum theoretical delta: adapt the **Lacasse et al. 2006 Cq/Ct** majority-vote bound (Gibbs mean+variance → majority-vote risk) to the fquad/flambda objectives.
2. **Exogenous-difficulty slack decomposition.** The original "difficulty flips the winner" idea is **circular** (difficulty measured only via the slack it explains → tautology). Formalize difficulty **exogenously** (label-noise rate / k-NN class-overlap / intrinsic dimension, measured from raw data) and **regress certificate slack on it** with a pre-registered difficulty×objective interaction test.
3. **Fashion-MNist = missing middle rung**, turning the MNIST→CIFAR-10 degradation into a 3-point ladder with a *fitted* accuracy-vs-tightness-vs-data-efficiency tradeoff curve — not stamp-collecting.

Plus one **3-axis interaction cell** (objective × predictor-rule × prior) no prior paper fills, and one **small analytical contribution** (KL-vs-empirical-risk slack decomposition vs difficulty) to convert "sweep" → "mechanism."

## The 8 must-fix items (from the cross-model kill-argument)
1. **Formalize difficulty exogenously** + regress slack on it with a difficulty×objective interaction test + CIs. Non-negotiable; without it the headline is circular.
2. **Drop all in-distribution claims** (objective ordering on MNIST/CIFAR, learnt-vs-isotropic in-distribution) to a single reproduction-validation row. Cite 2007.12911 + 2109.10304 as occupying that ground.
